Why Vidyarambh

Shaping Future Thinkers, Not Just Followers

We're redefining early education in Bodh Gaya through care, play and a scientific, child-first approach.

Our Philosophy

Inspired by NEP 2020, we believe the "Foundational Stage" is the most important period of a child's life — and we design every hour of the day around that belief.

The Happiness Quotient

We don't just track grades; we track smiles. A happy, secure child is a natural learner, so wellbeing comes before worksheets.

Your Child's Second Home

What We Offer

A safe, sanitised and stimulating environment, tailored for early childhood development.

Safe Spaces

Child-friendly classrooms with round-the-clock CCTV monitoring for total peace of mind.

Activity Zones

Dedicated areas for sand play, gardening and creative arts, right on campus.

Expert Educators

NEP-trained teachers who understand child psychology and facilitate rather than instruct.

Holistic Reports

Progress cards that reflect skills, behaviour and creativity — not just marks.

Curriculum

How We Implement NEP 2020

Every day at Vidyarambh is built on the National Curriculum Framework for the Foundational Stage.

Play-Based Learning

Toys, puzzles and games that build cognitive and motor skills without children realising they're "studying".

Multilingualism

We respect the mother tongue while gently introducing English, so every child feels at home while they learn.

No Rigid Boundaries

Science, art and play are woven together — your child might learn maths through music or storytelling.

Socio-Emotional Focus

Empathy, teamwork and ethics are inculcated from day one, alongside academics.

Our "No Heavy Bag" PolicyAll learning materials stay at school, so children travel light — and happy.
